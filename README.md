# Near & Far Weddings Launch

Create a one-page website for a new wedding planning business called Near & Far Weddings.

Use the attached copy in 1. Hero (pasted).md.

Use the attached photo IMG_6916.JPG for the About Me headshot, and the attached logomark IMG_9488.PNG for the logo (centered in the footer). Other accent photos can be stock images that fit the brand and make sense for weddings, preferably details like lace or abstracts like flower arrangements.

Brand: romantic vintage art deco with cosmic desert influences.
Color palette: Warm desert parchment and soft ivory canvas, deep midnight navy typography, muted terracotta accents, and delicate celestial gold highlights.

Flow and interaction details:
- For Offering 1 (Wedding Plan Coach, $150), the "Book Your Session →" button links out to an external booking link placeholder.
- For Offering 2 (Day-of Coordination) and Offering 3 (Full Service), the "Inquire →" buttons scroll down to the intake form with the selected package pre-populated.
- Include the warm welcome note, about section with Kelsey's headshot, testimonials placeholder block, the 6-field intake form with toast confirmation, and the centered logomark in the footer with tagline and contact details.
